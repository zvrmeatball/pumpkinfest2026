📡 Getting to 2m accuracy

The receiver reports accuracy = radius of error. Smaller = better.

On your phone (biggest win)

  1. Stand outdoors in open sky — away from buildings, trees, and walls. The school fields are ideal.
  2. Enable "high accuracy" / GPS + network in Location settings (not "battery saving").
  3. Let it settle 5–15 seconds before moving — accuracy tightens as more satellites lock.
  4. Hold the phone still toward the sky, pointed up, for best signal.
  5. Disable Bluetooth/Wi-Fi scanning if the dot keeps jumping (can swap location sources).

Environment matters

  1. 2m is only reachable with clear sky + high-accuracy mode. Indoors or near tall walls it falls to 10–50m.
  2. Clouds/rain rarely hurt GPS, but metal roofs and densely packed trees do.
  3. If you're near the school buildings, step away from the walls.

How to read the color

  1. Green < 3m — excellent (best for pinning spots)
  2. Yellow 3–8m — good, typical for phones
  3. Orange > 8m — poor; recheck sky/exposure

*Browser apps can't read the raw satellite count (not exposed by the OS/browser). Quality is estimated from the accuracy the device reports.
